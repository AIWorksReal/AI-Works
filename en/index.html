Frequently asked questions
How long does it take to build a website?

We can usually prepare a simple website within 3 business days, depending on the scope and how quickly content is provided.

Will my website appear on Google?

We implement basic SEO and prepare your website for indexing by Google. Search visibility depends on Google's indexing process.

Can you make changes after launch?

Yes, we can help with small changes after launch.

Do you help with domains and hosting?

Yes, we can recommend solutions and help with the technical setup.

What do you need to get started?

A short description of your business, services and contact details is enough. Photos and examples you like are helpful too.
